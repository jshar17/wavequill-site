Support

WaveQuill records meetings on your Mac and transcribes them locally. If you have a question, a bug to report, or a request, email wavequillsupport@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a person.

Before you write in

  • No screens or windows listed? macOS needs Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Grant it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and reopen WaveQuill — macOS only applies the change on relaunch.
  • Recording is silent? Internal meeting audio captures what your Mac plays. If your system output is routed to a virtual audio device, use the speaker selector so you can still hear the meeting.
  • Where are my recordings? The hero card shows the folder under SAVING TO. Change it with File › Choose Recording Library.
  • Transcript looks wrong? Transcripts come from a speech model running on your Mac. It makes mistakes, and on silence it can produce text that was never spoken. Check the recording before relying on a transcript.

Recording other people

Recording laws vary, and in some places everyone in a conversation must consent. You are responsible for knowing what applies to you and for telling participants that recording is on. WaveQuill shows a reminder before your first recording and may show it again when the notice changes; that reminder is not legal advice.

Your data

Recordings, transcripts and meeting names stay on your Mac. There is no account and no server of ours. See the privacy policy for the detail, including the one outbound request the app makes.
