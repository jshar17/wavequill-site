Privacy Policy for WaveQuill

Effective date: 25 July 2026

Joseph Sharpe ("we," "us," or "our") provides WaveQuill, a macOS application for recording and transcribing meetings.

Summary

WaveQuill is designed to keep meeting content on your Mac. We do not operate an account service, analytics service, advertising service, or cloud storage service for the app. We do not receive your recordings, imported media, transcripts, meeting names, or selected device information.

The app makes one kind of outbound connection: downloading the local transcription model described below. It has no account service and no server of ours to talk to. Your recordings, audio and transcripts are never sent anywhere.

Information stored on your Mac

At your direction, the app may create and store:

  • screen, application, or window video;
  • internal system audio from the source you select;
  • audio from a microphone you select;
  • locally generated transcripts and subtitle files;
  • session metadata such as the meeting name, recording settings, and time; and
  • a macOS security-scoped bookmark for the Recording Library folder you select.

These files are stored in the Recording Library you choose. If you do not choose another folder, the Mac App Store version stores its library in the app's sandboxed Application Support container. You control retention and may delete session folders in Finder.

Local transcription and model downloads

Transcription is performed on your Mac using WhisperKit. When you first use a transcription quality level, the app may connect to a Hugging Face-hosted Argmax model repository to download the required model files. The app does not upload your meeting audio, video, or transcript as part of that request. Network providers and the model-hosting service may process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and request metadata, under their own policies. Downloaded model files are stored locally in the app's Application Support container.

Permissions

The app requests only permissions needed for features you choose:

  • Screen & System Audio Recording: to list and capture the display, application, or window you select and its internal audio.
  • Microphone: to include audio from the input device you select.
  • User-selected files and folders: to use a Recording Library, import previous media, and export edited media.
  • Calendar: only if you turn on calendar auto-start, to see when an event with a video-call link is about to begin.
  • Outgoing network access: to download local transcription model files. This is the only outbound request the app makes.

You can change microphone and screen-recording permissions in macOS System Settings. You can change the Recording Library from the app's File menu.

Data collection, sharing, and sale

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share meeting content or personal information through the app. The current app includes no advertising or analytics SDK. If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before distributing the changed version.

Security

The macOS App Sandbox limits the app's access to system resources and user files. No software can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your Mac account and any Recording Library placed on shared, removable, or network storage.

Recording responsibility

You are responsible for telling participants when recording is active and for obtaining any consent required by law, workplace policy, contract, or the meeting service. The app does not determine whether a particular recording is lawful.

Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
